My Day Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers My Day, an app by Pare Apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and for Android phones and tablets. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Data on your devices

My Day shows your day using data that is already on your device. It only reads what you allow, and you can change that any time in your device's settings.

  • Calendar and Reminders. My Day reads your events (and, on iPhone, your reminders) to show them on your timeline and in its widgets, and saves the changes you make — such as a new event, a moved reminder or an edited title — back to your calendars and reminder lists. On Android, reminders you create in My Day are kept in the app on your device.
  • Health. With My Day Pro, My Day reads health data such as steps, sleep, heart rate, activity, VO₂ Max, HRV and workouts — from Apple Health on iPhone, and from Health Connect on Android — to show them on your timeline and in its widgets. It does not write any health data. Health data stays on your devices — on iPhone, My Day passes a summary to your Apple Watch to show there — and is never sent to us or to anyone else, never synced to the cloud, and never used for advertising.
  • Photos. With My Day Pro, My Day shows the photos you took on each day. They are read on your device and never uploaded by My Day. To show where photos were taken, My Day sends their location to your device's mapping service (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android) to get the place's name.
  • Journal. Journal entries you write in My Day are kept on your device, and in the cloud only as described below.

We do not collect your calendar, reminder, health or photo data, and it is not used to track you.

iCloud (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch)

To-dos you create in My Day, the days you save, your goals and your journal entries are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot see, so they stay in step across your devices. Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted: only your devices signed in to your Apple Account can read them.

Your My Day settings (what the day shows, its layout, units, the week's first day, Insights' layout, how reminder lists are sorted, and which calendars you've chosen, with each calendar's name and account so the right one is picked on your other devices) are kept in your own iCloud key-value storage, which we also cannot see, so a reinstall or another device comes up set the same way.

Your iCloud data is kept until you delete it. Settings › Privacy › Delete All Journal Entries deletes every journal entry from your devices and from iCloud, and you can remove all of My Day's iCloud data in your Apple Account's iCloud settings.

Exporting and importing (iPhone and iPad)

Settings › Privacy › Export My Data saves your journal entries, goals, saved days and My Day settings to a file, after you confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The file is not encrypted: anyone who has a copy can read your journal. It goes only where you choose to save or send it, such as Files or AirDrop; it is never sent to us. Import Data reads such a file back in on your device.

Cloud Sync (Android)

Cloud Sync is off until you turn it on in My Day's Settings. It keeps your journal entries, the reminders you make in My Day, the days you save and your My Day settings in the cloud under your Google account, so they're on every Android device where you sign in to My Day with that account, and come back after you reinstall My Day or move to a new phone.

  • What's stored. Your journal entries (the day, title, words, mood, when each was last changed, and how long you spent writing and reading it), your reminders and their lists (each reminder's title, notes, list, due date and time, and whether and when you completed it; each list's name, colour and order), your saved days, and your My Day settings (what the day shows, units, the week's first day, Insights' layout, and which calendars you've hidden, stored as a one-way fingerprint of each calendar's name and account, not the name itself). Nothing else: not your calendar events, health data or photos.
  • Your account. You sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication. Firebase receives your Google account's email address, name and a user ID, which identify your data in the cloud. We don't use them for anything else.
  • Who can read it. Your data is readable only when signed in to your account: security rules stop any other account from reading or changing it. It's encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. It isn't end-to-end encrypted, so it's stored in a form Google's systems can read, and Pare Apps can technically access it through our Firebase console; we don't look at it, and only do so if you ask us to help with your data.
  • Who stores it. Your data is stored by Google's Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), in the United States, which processes it on our behalf. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • How long it's kept. Until you delete it. Turning Cloud Sync off stops syncing but leaves your cloud data for your other devices. A journal entry or reminder you delete is cleared from the cloud, leaving only a note that it was deleted (a journal entry's day, a reminder's random ID), so your other devices remove it too.
  • Deleting it. In My Day's Settings, Delete My Cloud Data deletes everything Cloud Sync keeps in the cloud and your Cloud Sync account, right away. What's on your device stays. You can also email us to have it deleted; here's how.

Purchases

My Day Pro is bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions: it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple or Google so the app knows whether Pro is active. We never see your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics and crash reports

On iPhone and iPad, My Day uses Google's Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used: which features are used and how (for example, that an event was moved or a screen was opened with a swipe, the buttons tapped, the layout the screen was in, and whether you have My Day Pro), plus device and app information (such as model, iOS version and app version), tied to a random identifier rather than to you. It never receives what you wrote or recorded: no event or reminder titles, notes, places, list names, journal words, or health or photo data. My Day doesn't use the advertising identifier or track you across other companies' apps. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data; it's also off in Demo Mode. See Firebase's privacy information.

On Android, My Day uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics the same way: which features are used and how (for example, that a reminder was added from the timeline or a panel was opened with a swipe), device and app information, and crash logs, tied to random identifiers. They never receive what you wrote or recorded: no event or reminder titles, notes, places, list names, journal words or health data. My Day for Android doesn't use an advertising ID. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Data; it's also off in Demo Mode.

Children

My Day is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off any permission in your device's settings, turn off Share Usage Data, delete your journal entries, turn Cloud Sync off or delete your cloud data in My Day's Settings, and deleting My Day removes its data from your device. To ask about your data, or to have it deleted, email us at developer@pareapps.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
