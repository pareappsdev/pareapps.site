My Day Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy covers My Day, an iPhone app by Pare Apps. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Data on your iPhone

My Day shows your day using data that is already on your iPhone. It only reads what you allow, and you can change that any time in the Settings app.

  • Calendar and Reminders. My Day reads your events and reminders to show them on your timeline and in its widgets, and saves the changes you make — such as a new event, a moved reminder or an edited title — back to your calendars and reminder lists.
  • Health. With My Day Pro, My Day reads health data such as steps, sleep, heart rate, activity rings, VO₂ Max, HRV and workouts to show them on your timeline and in its widgets. It does not write anything to Health. Health data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.
  • Photos. With My Day Pro, My Day shows the photos you took on each day. They are read on your device and never uploaded by My Day. To show where photos were taken, My Day sends their location to Apple's mapping service to get the place's name.
  • iCloud. To-dos you create in My Day are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot see.

We do not collect your calendar, reminder, health or photo data, and it is not used to track you.

Purchases

My Day Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions: it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple so the app knows whether Pro is active. We never see your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics, crash reports and settings

My Day uses Google's Firebase to understand how the app is used, to fix crashes, and to adjust some settings without an update. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config collect usage events, device and app information (such as model, iOS version and app version) and crash logs, tied to random identifiers rather than to you. They never receive your calendar, reminder, health or photo data. See Firebase's privacy information.

Children

My Day is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off any permission in the Settings app, and deleting My Day removes its data from your device. To ask about your data, or to have it deleted, email us at developer@pareapps.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
