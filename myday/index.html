My Day

Your calendar, reminders, workouts, health and photos — together on one timeline.

Coming soon to the App Store

Your whole day, at a glance

Every day is one timeline. Your meetings, the reminders that are due, the run you went on and the photos you took all sit at the time they happened. Swipe between days, or jump back to today in one tap.

Quick cards

Tap anything for the details

Tap an event and it opens right where it is: the time, place, calendar, notes and any photos you took during it. Get directions, join the call, move it to later or to tomorrow, or edit it — without leaving your day.

Photos

Turn photos into memories

Photos taken outside of any event are grouped on your timeline — “3 Photos and 1 Video” — with where you took them. Tap them to make an event of the moment: My Day fills in the time and place from the photos and saves it to your calendar, with the photos inside.

Workouts

Your workouts, with the trends

Workouts from Apple Health appear on your timeline. Tap one for its duration, distance, pace, calories and heart rate — and 30 days of distance with your VO₂ Max drawn over it.

Reminders

Reminders, one swipe away

Swipe in from the right edge to bring your reminders over your day. Long-press a reminder on the timeline and drag it to a new time.

Private

Built on what you already use

My Day reads from Apple's Calendar, Reminders, Health and Photos — no new account, nothing to import. You choose what it can see, and can change your mind any time in Settings.
