My Day

Your calendar, reminders, workouts, health and photos — together on one timeline, on iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

Your whole day, at a glance

Every day is one timeline. Your meetings, the reminders that are due, the run you went on and the photos you took all sit at the time they happened. Swipe between days, or jump back to today in one tap.

Quick cards

Tap anything for the details

Tap an event and it opens right where it is: the time, place, calendar, notes and the photos you took during it. Get directions, join the call, or swipe the card to move it an hour or a day. Long-press an empty spot on your timeline to start a new event right there.

Photos

Your photos, where they happened

Photos you took during an event sit inside it. The rest are grouped on your timeline with the place you took them, and one tap makes an event of the moment, with the time and place filled in. Open a photo to see it full screen, with the camera and lens that took it, and share it from there.

Workouts

Your workouts, with the trends

Workouts from Apple Health appear on your timeline, years back. Tap one for its duration, distance, pace, calories, heart rate and VO₂ Max — and 30 days of distance with your VO₂ Max drawn over it. Details & Trends goes further: by the week, month or year, with each bar's total.

Insights

How your day really went

Swipe in from the left edge for Insights: your rings, steps, sleep, resting heart rate, VO₂ Max and HRV, each with when it was last measured and how it is trending. Tap any of them for 30 days of history. Below sit your streaks, what's coming up, and the invitations waiting for a reply.

Favorite days

Keep the days that mattered

Pull up past the end of a day to make it a favorite. Each one becomes a card with what happened, your workouts and how many photos you took — counting the days since, or down to the ones still ahead. Favorites stay in step across your devices through iCloud.

Private

Built on what you already use

My Day reads from Apple's Calendar, Reminders, Health and Photos — no new account, nothing to import. You choose what it can see, and can change your mind any time in Settings. Your end-of-day journal is end-to-end encrypted in your own iCloud.

iPad

Room for your whole day

On iPad, Insights, your day and your reminders sit side by side. Bring them in from either edge, use the pointer on anything that opens a card, and turn your iPad either way.
