Delete Your My Day Data

Last updated November 14, 2026

My Day for Android keeps your journal entries, saved days and settings on your device. If you turned on Cloud Sync, it also keeps them in the cloud under a Cloud Sync account linked to your Google account. This page explains how to delete that cloud data and the account. My Day for iPhone keeps its data in your own iCloud, which you manage in your Apple Account settings.

In the app

  1. Open My Day and tap the gear at the top to open Settings.
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete My Cloud Data, and confirm.

This deletes your journal entries, saved days and settings from the cloud, and your Cloud Sync account, right away. Everything on your device stays. Your other devices stop syncing and keep what they have.

Without the app

If you no longer have My Day installed, email developer@pareapps.com from the Google account you used for Cloud Sync, and ask us to delete your data. We'll delete your cloud data and your Cloud Sync account within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Deleted: your journal entries (titles, words, moods and the time spent writing and reading them), your saved days, your synced settings, and your Cloud Sync account (the email address, name and user ID it holds).
  • Not kept: nothing from Cloud Sync is kept after deletion. Purchase records for My Day Pro are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as their own policies require, and aren't linked to your Cloud Sync account.

See the My Day Privacy Policy for everything My Day collects and how it's used.
