Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email developer@pareapps.com and we'll get back to you.

My Day

Nothing shows on my timeline. My Day needs your permission for each thing it shows. Open the Settings app, find My Day, and turn on Calendars, Reminders, Health and Photos as you like.

How do I see my reminders? Swipe in from the right edge of the screen.

How do I move a reminder? Long-press it on the timeline and drag it to a new time.

How do I manage or cancel My Day Pro? In My Day, open Settings and tap Manage Subscription — or go to your Apple Account's Subscriptions in the Settings app.

I bought Pro on another device. In My Day, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

PhonePare

A phone is missing or a spec looks wrong. Email us the phone and what's off, and we'll fix it. Changes reach the app without an update.

How do I compare phones? Tap the compare button at the top of the list, pick two or three phones, then tap Compare. Differences shows only the rows where they differ.

Why do some links say PhonePare may earn a commission? Some store links are affiliate links. Buying through one costs you nothing extra and helps support the app.
