My Day

Nothing shows on my timeline. My Day needs your permission for each thing it shows. Open the Settings app, find My Day, and turn on Calendars, Reminders, Health and Photos as you like.

How do I see my reminders? Swipe in from the right edge of the screen.

How do I move a reminder? Long-press it on the timeline and drag it to a new time.

How do I manage or cancel My Day Pro? In My Day, open Settings and tap Manage Subscription — or go to your Apple Account's Subscriptions in the Settings app.

I bought Pro on another device. In My Day, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
