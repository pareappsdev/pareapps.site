SavePare Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy covers SavePare, an iPhone app by Pare Apps. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Your costs

The subscriptions and costs you enter in SavePare are kept on your device. We do not collect them.

Children

SavePare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off any permission in the Settings app, and deleting SavePare removes its data from your device. To ask about your data, or to have it deleted, email us at developer@pareapps.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
