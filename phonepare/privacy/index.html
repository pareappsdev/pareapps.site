PhonePare Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers PhonePare, an app by Pare Apps for iPhone and for Android phones and tablets. PhonePare has no accounts and asks for no permissions. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Phone information

PhonePare downloads its list of phones, their specs, prices and photos from our servers, which run on Google's Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). Like any connection over the internet, these requests include your device's IP address, which Google uses to deliver the data. We don't store it or tie it to you. See Firebase's privacy information.

On your device

PhonePare keeps a copy of the phone list on your device so it opens quickly and works offline, along with how you last sorted, filtered and laid out the list. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

App Check

To keep other apps and scripts from using our servers, PhonePare uses Firebase App Check. It asks your device to confirm that requests come from the real PhonePare app — through Apple's App Attest on iPhone and Google Play Integrity on Android — and sends that confirmation with each request. It doesn't include your name or any information about you.

Analytics

PhonePare uses Google's Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, so we can make it better: the screens you open, the phones you view, compare or swipe through photos of, the words you search for, how you sort and filter the list, and the store links and review videos you tap. It also receives device and app information, such as the model, system version and app version, and your approximate region. All of this is tied to a random identifier for the app on your device, not to you. PhonePare doesn't use the advertising identifier and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Review videos

Phones can show review videos from YouTube. Their thumbnails load from YouTube's servers, which receive your IP address and device information like any web request. Tapping a video opens it in the YouTube app or your browser, where Google's privacy policy applies.

Store links and affiliate links

Tapping a store link opens that store's website or app, such as Amazon or Best Buy, where its own privacy policy applies. Some of these links are affiliate links: if you buy something through one, Pare Apps may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. To credit the sale, the store or its affiliate network (such as Amazon Associates or Impact) may use cookies or similar technology on its own site. We only ever see totals of qualifying sales and commissions, never who bought what.

Children

PhonePare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

PhonePare works without any account or personal information. Deleting the app removes everything it keeps on your device. To ask about your data, email us at developer@pareapps.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
