WeatherPare Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy covers WeatherPare, an iPhone app by Pare Apps. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Location

WeatherPare uses the places you add, and your current location if you allow it, to look up the weather there. Those locations are sent to a weather service only to fetch forecasts and past weather, and are not used to track you. You can turn off location access any time in the Settings app.

Your places

The list of places you add is kept on your device.

Children

WeatherPare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off any permission in the Settings app, and deleting WeatherPare removes its data from your device. To ask about your data, or to have it deleted, email us at developer@pareapps.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
